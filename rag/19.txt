                        Ketose test :
Procedure :
1) Add 2 ml of sugar solution to 1 ml of conc HCl solution.
2) Boil for 2 min.
Observation:
Glucose: Colorless.
Fructose: Reddish brown.
Comment:
In case of fructose:
 Dehydration of fructose and formation of furfural which is reddish brown
 in color in acidic medium.
In case of glucose:
  Dehydration to hydroxymethyl furfural (HMF) which is colorless in
   acidic medium.
