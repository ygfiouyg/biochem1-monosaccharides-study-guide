                Carbohydrates
1. Definition:

   Carbohydrates are organic substances containing C, H, and O.
   They are polyhydroxyaldehyde or polyhydroxyketones,
   or any compound giving these substances on hydrolysis

 2. Function:
A. Provide energy in the diet of most organisms.
B. Acting as a storage form of energy in the body
C. Serve as cell membrane components that mediate some forms of
   intercellular communication
D. Serve as a structural component of many organisms, including
   the cell walls of bacteria, the exoskeleton of many insects and
   the fibrous cellulose of plants.
