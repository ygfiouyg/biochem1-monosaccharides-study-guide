 A. Molisch's reaction:
General Reactions for all Carbohydrates.
Procedure :
1)   Place about 2 ml of sugar solution in a test tube.
2)   Add a 1-2 drops of alcoholic α-naphthol.
3)   Add 2 ml of conc. sulfuric acid carefully on the wall of the tube & don’t shake.
Observation:
Violet ring at the junction of the 2 layers spreads by shaking.
Comment:
1.   Dehydration of sugar to furfural in fructose or hydroxymethyl furfural (HMF) in
     glucose by conc sulphuric.
2.   Condensation of HMF with 2 molecules of alpha naphthol giving violet ring.
